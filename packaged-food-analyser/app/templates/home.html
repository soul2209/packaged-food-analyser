From a packet to a score in three steps

  1. Photograph the label

    Take a photo of the ingredients and nutrition table, type a barcode, or paste the text. Barcodes in the photo are picked up automatically.

  2. We read and sort it

    Tesseract reads the text. We split the ingredient list, find E-numbers, and turn the nutrition table into values per 100 g, even when the pack prints per serving.

  3. Get a score you can act on

    The report shows the score, what lost points, which additives and allergens were found, and how it compares with any other product you have scanned.

Where the score comes from
